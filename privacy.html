Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version CANTO does not collect, store, or sell your data. Audio never reaches CANTO's servers — speech recognition runs entirely inside your browser, processed by Google (Chrome) or Microsoft (Edge). The only things sent over the network are the text of what you said, for translation and text-to-speech.

Speech Recognition

CANTO uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API. When you speak, your audio is sent directly from your browser to the speech recognition service operated by your browser vendor:

  • Google Chrome — processed by Google's speech servers
  • Microsoft Edge — processed by Microsoft's speech servers

CANTO receives only the resulting text transcript. Raw audio is never transmitted to CANTO. Processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Microsoft's Privacy Statement respectively.

Translation

Transcribed text is sent to the MyMemory free translation API. Only the spoken text is transmitted — no user identifiers, no account data. MyMemory's privacy policy governs that request.

Text-to-Speech (Speak Mode)

In Speak mode, the Cantonese translation is sent to Google Translate's text-to-speech endpoint to generate audio. Only the translated phrase is transmitted. No audio is recorded or stored server-side.

Local Storage

CANTO stores one item in your browser's localStorage:

  • canto_seen — a flag indicating you have seen the welcome guide

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time via your browser's developer tools or site settings.

Server Logs

CANTO is hosted on Vercel. Vercel's infrastructure may retain standard HTTP access logs (IP address, timestamp, request path) for a limited period in accordance with Vercel's Privacy Policy. CANTO does not access or process these logs.

Cookies & Analytics

CANTO does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics of any kind.

Children

CANTO is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be updated. Continued use of CANTO after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact
